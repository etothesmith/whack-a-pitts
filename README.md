# Whack-a-Pitts: The Revenge of Dr. Caliari

A 7-level browser gag game: Assistant Principal Dr. Caliari vs. the chaotic PE coach, Coach Pitts.

**Play:** https://etothesmith.github.io/whack-a-pitts/

## Levels
1. **The Classic Whack-a-Pitts** – bop Pitts out of the lockers (don't hit the Principal or the Custodian!)
2. **The Admin vs. Athletics Dual Meet** – a three-period high school wrestling match
3. **Hallway Hurdle Havoc** – 3-lane hallway runner
4. **The Work Order Stampede** – stamp tickets: real emergency or Pitts excuse?
5. **The Great Equipment Roundup** – clean up the gym before tip-off
6. **In-Service Stealth Escape** – sneak past Pitts' flashlight cones
7. **The Field Day Fiasco** – 3-phase golf-cart boss battle

Works with keyboard, mouse or touch. On phones: play in landscape, use the thumb stick on the left, and add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone) to play full screen like an app. Progress, stars and high scores save in your browser.
Secret: type `WORKORDER` (or tap the star counter on the level select 5 times) for Infinite Coffee Mode.

## Files
- `index.html` – the whole game (no libraries; all sounds synthesized)
- `assets/` – cartoon sprites, icons and backgrounds. Swap any file (same name) to change the art,
  or edit the `ASSETS` list at the top of `index.html`. Missing images fall back to built-in drawings.

To play offline: download the repo and double-click `index.html`.
